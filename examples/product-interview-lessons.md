3 things I learnt from product interviews over the last month that might help you if you have one scheduled next week.

1. assignment discussion round is about rationalising every decision you made, I made a few rookie mistakes here. 

What might help is sitting with your assignment and asking yourself why? for every action.

2. Show your AI work. Share your screen and walk the interviewer through what you’ve built instead of a verbal explanation.

3. Build more evidence of product growth: everyone is not lying when they say distribution is the moat.
